# Independent audits and feedback

Independent checks are welcome, whether they support, restrict or contradict a stated result. Begin with [REPRODUCING.md](REPRODUCING.md) and the exact package guide. Keep the originals unchanged and create fresh outputs in a separate directory.

Open one [GitHub issue](https://github.com/jambormartinsvk-netizen/cellular-universe/issues/new/choose) per distinct finding. Include:

- Commit/tag, case and precise claim or equation; source and evidence hashes.
- Your method, Python/dependency versions, commands, fresh outputs and uncertainty or comparison rules.
- Your conclusion: agree in scope, agree with limitations, disagree, or cannot determine.
- **FRAME_CHALLENGE:** does this calculation test the physical claim being made? Which assumptions and physical sectors are actually covered?
- Where the reasoning first fails, if applicable; which descendants it affects and which results survive.
- Whether this is your own human review or a model-assisted/model-only review; name the model and disclose material assistance.

A code patch may accompany the finding, but do not replace accepted evidence with your rerun. Link existing issues for repeated root causes instead of creating duplicate reports. Avoid posting credentials, unrelated personal information or proprietary material.

The maintainer records one scoped response and triages material findings through the internal workflow. External review can remain pending while valid work continues. An issue or model agreement does not automatically accept or reject the theory. Final verification in the project requires at least three documented independent qualified human physics authorities for the same claim, scope and evidence, with no open material objections; language models count as zero.

The invitation to review makes no promise of response time or of eventual confirmation. Helpful feedback includes a clear limitation or counterexample, not only a completed reproduction.
