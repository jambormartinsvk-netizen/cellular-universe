# Addendum to 05 — particle-production moments (EN)

## AR46 — a scalar number source is not a complete energy-momentum transfer

The equation `nabla_mu N^mu=S_n` fixes the zeroth production moment. By itself it does not fix the orthogonal part of `Q^mu`, the birth frame, the pressure moment, or noise. A particle-production track must not pass G2/G3 until it explicitly specifies the required moments of the collision kernel or an equivalent covariant closure.

An effective creation pressure is an alternative rewriting of an explicit energy source. The same contribution must not be counted both as `p_cr` and as an unchanged explicit `Q^mu`.

This rule does not duplicate AR45: AR45 prohibits counting a constraint used for evolution as an independent test; AR46 prohibits replacing a full transfer operator by one kinetic moment and prohibits double bookkeeping of a source.

## Q72 — which collision kernel can close constant-mass ash production?

**Status:** OPEN.

It must determine the zeroth moment `S_n`, the first moment `Q_c^mu`, the pressure/anisotropic stress of the produced ash, fuel backreaction, entropy, and noise. Cold closures `Q || u_c` and `Q || u_f` are already covered by dead K1 and K3; `Q || u_d` merges with K4.

## Limitation of the older wording

The older `Q_c=m_c S_n` is valid as an FLRW energy scalar or as the projection `-u_c.Q_c=m_cS_n`. It is not a complete four-vector equation without the additional condition `a_c^mu=0`. The older requirement to “add creation pressure” is limited to “derive it and choose one consistent bookkeeping convention.”
