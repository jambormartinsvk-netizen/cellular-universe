# REGISTER 05 — EN record of the readable A2 track catalog

**Date:** 2026-07-13  
**Status:** binding addendum; existing rules are unchanged

## Rule duplicate check

No new rule is added. The original TRACK BIRTH rule already requires a
differentiation statement, and AR10 assigns a new peer-level number when the
mechanism changes. This addendum only closes documentation question Q39.

## Q39 — Where are the foundations and differences of K1–K10 recorded in readable form?

**Status:** `DOCUMENTATION CLOSED`.

The canonical summary is
`Audit/A2_KATALOG_KOLAJI_K1_AZ_K10_ZROZUMITELNY_SUMAR.md`. For every track it
records:

- the original physical hypothesis;
- a plain-language physical picture;
- its difference from adjacent tracks;
- a “what this track is not” statement;
- its status and death reason or next kill gate;
- the path to the deep evidence when a retrospective audit is needed.

## Restriction of older summaries

Tables listing only `Q parallel u_A`, a mode number, or a death reason remain
historically correct but are no longer a sufficient canonical description of
a track. In particular, A2-K1 must be described as energy transfer in the CDM
rest frame: CDM receives no momentum kick there, while the near-vacuum fuel
carries the recoil. This foundation explains its death and prevents the same
model from returning under a new name.

The catalog must be checked before creating A2-K11 or any further subtrack.
