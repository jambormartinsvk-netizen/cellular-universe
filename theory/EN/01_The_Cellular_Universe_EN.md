## 1. The question posed by the theory

General relativity describes spacetime as continuous geometry and precisely
specifies how matter affects its metric. Quantum field theory describes
particles and their interactions. The standard cosmological model describes
cosmic expansion, relic radiation, and structure growth using general
relativity, cold dark matter, and a cosmological constant. It therefore also
includes the observed accelerated expansion of the late Universe. These
theories are experimentally successful in their validated domains. Quantum
Cellular Theory of Space must not bypass them or replace them with figurative
language. It asks a deeper question:

> **Can observed physics be the effective surface trace of local, discrete,
> and nonequilibrium dynamics of space?**

### 1.1 The central working bridge of the theory

Quantum Cellular Theory of Space does not yet have one derived microscopic
“equation of everything”. Its central currently used bridge between network
geometry and the cosmological description is the effective rearrangement
overhead

$$
\boxed{
\delta=\frac{1}{\langle k\rangle+C}
}
\tag{1}
$$

and its conditional numerical evaluation

$$
\langle k\rangle=\frac{48\pi^2}{35}+2\simeq15.535,
\qquad
C=28,
\qquad
\boxed{\delta\simeq0.02297}.
\tag{2}
$$

**Meaning of the symbols:**

- $\delta$ is the dimensionless effective cost or dissipative overhead of
  one rearrangement;
- $\langle k\rangle$ is the mean number of surface neighbours in the
  reference three-dimensional Poisson–Delaunay network;
- $C$ is the working internal capacity of a cell; the value `28` is read as
  the number of bosonic states in the restored phase of the Standard Model.

**Relation to mainstream physics:** equation (1) is not part of general
relativity, the Standard Model, or ΛCDM. Mainstream physics may specify a
cosmological constant or a dark-energy equation of state, but it does not
derive the parameter $\delta$ from the connectivity of discrete space. QCTS
differs precisely in its attempt to connect network geometry to the effective
pressure of fuel,

$$
p_f=(-1+\delta)\rho_f,
\qquad
w_f\equiv\frac{p_f}{\rho_f}=-1+\delta.
\tag{3}
$$

Here $\rho_f$ and $p_f$ denote the density and pressure of fuel, and $w_f$
its equation-of-state parameter. Equation (3) has the same fluid form used
in modern cosmology; the proposed microscopic origin
$w_f+1=\delta$ is different.

**Status:** the geometric expression for $\langle k\rangle$ and its network
simulations have limited mathematical support. The identification $C=28$
and the step from one rearrangement to equation (3) are
`CONDITIONAL_MECHANISM_READING`, not a proved microdynamical theorem. The
numerical value $\delta=0.02297$ is therefore a working input of the live
track, not an experimentally confirmed constant of nature.

There is not yet a direct sealed external package that verifies the
microscopic bridge (1) as a physical law. The package cited at equations
(20)–(22) checks only its conditional use in early-background normalisation;
it does not prove the origin of $C$ or $\delta$.

### 1.3 How the proposal differs

The present distinctiveness of QCTS lies mainly in its ontology and causal
architecture:

1. space is not a fundamental continuous stage but the macroscopic limit of
   a network of local cells;
2. cosmological expansion is investigated as growth or rearrangement of the
   network, not as motion of cells into an external space;
3. “fuel”, “ash”, and “steam” are different bookkeeping outputs of one
   possible local process, not three arbitrarily added cosmological fluids;
4. the light cone, Lorentz symmetry, and gravity are intended to be emergent
   properties of a common substrate;
5. quantum measurement and the arrow of time are investigated as the
   formation of a physical scar in the network, not as a special role of
   consciousness.

A **scar** is not another name for ash or another cosmological fluid. It is a
candidate persistent change in a cell's internal state, its links, or a local
domain after a physical event. It entered the programme because, if QCTS is
to explain fundamental physical irreversibility rather than only its
effective description, it must identify a state carrier in which the event's
history remains. A purely reversible cellular update without such a declared
carrier would not yet realise this ambition. The exact origin of a scar has
not been derived: its trigger may be a transition, failure, absorption, or
cell rearrangement, and it is not yet known whether one mechanism accounts
for memory, objective collapse, and the arrow of time. These are open
questions `Q4` and `Q8`, not accepted results.

These points are research hypotheses. By themselves they do not prove that
such a substrate exists.

### 1.5 Working physical mechanism

The working answer is the hypothesis that space has microscopic local
degrees of freedom—cells—and that macroscopic expansion may be a limiting
manifestation of their rearrangement or division. A metastable energetic
component that permits this rearrangement is called **fuel**. Because its
working continuum description has pressure close to vacuum pressure, it is
also investigated as a possible source of a negative effective pressure
component and thus of accelerated expansion. This remains a hypothesis at
the homogeneous cosmological-background level; neither the microscopic
origin of fuel nor a complete observational fit has been derived.

The origin of matter entered the programme as a mandatory question for any
theory of a spatial substrate. Explaining only why cosmic distances change
is insufficient. The theory must also show why stable material excitations
exist and where energy and momentum go during rearrangement. The current
model has not derived Standard Model particles or the origin of baryonic
matter. It does investigate whether part of matter can be a persistent or
long-lived product of local network rearrangement.

The working names **ash** and **steam** were therefore not introduced as two
additional substances merely to improve agreement with data. They denote two
possible channels in a complete energy ledger:

- **ash** `c` is a candidate nonrelativistic, gravitationally clustering
  remnant of rearrangement and therefore a candidate for part of the
  physical role attributed to dark matter;
- **steam** $s$ is a candidate relativistic or free-streaming share of the
  transferred energy that might contribute to a radiative or thermal
  background.

Conservation requires a complete energy-momentum account, but does not by
itself determine that both channels must exist, what their shares are, or in
what order they arise. Parallel production, a sequential chain, and mixed
branching therefore remain open physical possibilities to be distinguished
by a local covariant law and observations.

Formulated in this way, the programme investigates whether one common
mechanism can connect:

1. the emergence of macroscopic space, its expansion, and late-time
   acceleration;
2. the origin of stable matter and an invisible clustering component;
3. a possible relativistic product and its trace in the early thermal
   background;
4. initial perturbations, the CMB, and subsequent growth of cosmic
   structures;
5. wave propagation of excitations, an emergent light cone, the Lorentz
   limit, and the gravitational limit of the network;
6. physical irreversibility, an event scar, and the arrow of time.

### 1.6 Light, wave behaviour, and a common limiting `c`

For light, the proposal has two distinct levels. In the audited scalar graph
operator, a plane wave $e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf x}$ may be used as a
variational trial state; on a random graph it is not generally an exact
eigenmode. The corresponding variational expression is even in
$\mathbf q$. In a separate simulation, the signal front broadens
sublinearly, so its relative width decreases. This provides a candidate
mechanism for transfer of a wave excitation. A sharp physical light cone
would follow only after showing that this scaling persists in the
macroscopic and continuum limits. The theory therefore proposes reading the
wave nature of light as propagation of a network excitation. This is not yet
a derivation of the electromagnetic field or proof that the photon is this
particular network wave.

The intended hypothesis for the same locally measured speed of light is
that light, matter, clocks, and rods are excitations of the same substrate
and should read the same limiting cone. A common substrate or capacity alone
does not prove this conclusion. If a common local Lorentzian metric, a single
limiting cone, and boost-covariant dynamics were derived for all these
excitations, transformations of clocks, rods, and signals would be mutually
consistent and inertial observers would locally measure the same `c`. This
is a conditional target of the hypothesis, not a closed proof: the photon
sector, common metric and universal coupling of all fields, boost symmetry,
absence of forbidden birefringence, and the equivalence principle still
have to be derived.

## 2. Physical picture and its causal ordering

The word cell does not mean a biological cell. It names a hypothetical
discrete local degree of freedom. A cell is not an object embedded in an
already existing space; instead, space is intended to emerge from cells and
their relations. A surface network determines local contacts along which
excitations may propagate. The proposed “interior” of a cell represents a
finite internal capacity, but its exact quantum realisation has not been
derived.

### 2.1 What a local event must do

The theory does not yet know the complete temporal order of all products. It
therefore does not present an invented linear chain, but only the **necessary
partial causal ordering**:

1. a cell and its surroundings have an initial state and locally available
   energy called fuel `f`;
2. a local law may permit a physical event—a transition, rearrangement, or
   division of the cell;
3. that event must simultaneously close its geometric and energy-momentum
   accounts: the network change carries the overhead `delta`, and energy may
   not disappear;
4. allowed outputs of the account are investigated as a stable clustering
   product `c` (ash), a relativistic product $s$ (steam), and possibly other
   material excitations; their parallel production, sequential chain, and
   mixed branching remain open;
5. the event **may** leave a persistent local change in the internal state or
   links—a scar/domain I. It has not been proved that a scar arises at every
   division, that it is energetically identical to ash, or that it selects a
   single outcome of a quantum measurement;
6. only the macroscopic limit of repeated events is intended to give
   expansion `H(a)`, while perturbations of the products should affect the
   CMB, structure growth, and lensing.

This ordering specifies only that the result cannot precede the local event
and that geometry, products, and any memory must share one consistent
account. It does not determine whether ash, steam, or a scar arises first.
That decision belongs to a local covariant production-transport operator and
observations. The current background model transfers energy from fuel to
CDM/ash and conserves baryons; it does not derive the production of ordinary
matter, steam, or a scar.

## 3. What “derived”, “conditional”, and “open” mean

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DERIVED` | derived in the precisely stated mathematical scope |
| `CONDITIONAL` | valid only for the stated inputs, anchor, or model |
| `HYPOTHESIS` | physically admissible proposal without proof of existence |
| `OPEN` | a mandatory derivation, mechanism, or calculation is not closed |
| `WITHDRAWN` | an older exact formulation is no longer used as a current claim |
| `HISTORICAL` | fully labelled older result without current predictive weight |
| `STOP_SCOPE` | certified contradiction only in the precisely stated subclass |

Several recurring technical words have a narrow meaning in this document.
`Scope` is the precisely bounded model class for which a result was actually
derived. A `comparator` tests whether a limited mechanism reproduces a known
scaling or number; it is not automatically a derivation of the whole
physics. A `background` is homogeneous cosmological evolution without
spatial perturbations. A `ledger` is a system of balance equations recording
where energy leaves and where it enters. `Closure` means that no required
equation or counter-source is missing at the stated descriptive level. An
`observable` is a quantity that can be compared with measurement through an
explicit map.

Failure to find a function, or an empty numerical grid by itself, is not a
proof of nonexistence. Until nonemptiness or emptiness of the common
admissible set has been proved, the state is
`GLOBAL_FEASIBILITY_INCOMPLETE`.

The philosophical basis can be summarised by five rules. Observable reality
may be an effective layer of a deeper substrate (F1), but an unobservable
object must have a quantifiable indirect trace (F2). Measurement should not
rely on privileged consciousness but on physical irreversibility (F3). The
model assigns no purpose to the Universe (F4). Local rearrangement must not
be free: it must have an energetic, entropic, or capacity cost (F5). F5
motivates `delta`, but does not itself prove its value.

The following chapters therefore do not begin with the conclusion but with
individual bridges that would have to connect the cellular hypothesis to

### 4.1 Poisson–Delaunay geometry

The geometric comparator used by the model is

$$
\boxed{
\langle k\rangle=\frac{48\pi^2}{35}+2\simeq15.535
}
\tag{5}
$$

**Symbols:** $k$ is the degree of one node, i.e. its number of neighbours;
$\langle k\rangle$ is the ensemble mean; $\pi$ is the circle constant.

**Mainstream and difference:** this is a known stereological result for
ideal three-dimensional Poisson–Delaunay geometry, not a new law of QCTS.
QCTS uses it as the geometric input to equation (1), thereby assigning it a

Archived simulations reported approximately `15.58` for a Poisson network

**Status in v3.18:** `CONDITIONAL / HISTORICAL_SUPPORT`. The comparator and
historical measurements remain usable in their exact network scope. They do

The macroscopic anisotropy limit remains open.

### 4.2 Division overhead

The model's conditional geometric reading is

$$
\boxed{
\delta=\frac{1}{\langle k\rangle+C}
}
\tag{6}
$$

For $\langle k\rangle=15.535$ and $C=28$, it gives

$$
\delta=\frac{1}{15.535+28}\simeq\boxed{0.02297}.
\tag{7}
$$

**Symbols:** $\delta$ is the dimensionless overhead; $\langle k\rangle$ the
number of surface contacts; and $C$ the working internal capacity.

**Mainstream and difference:** modern cosmology may parameterise
$w=-1+\delta$, but does not contain relation (6). The novelty of QCTS is the
hypothesis that a cost of local network rearrangement determines the
deviation from vacuum pressure.

The motivation is the share of one rearranged interface in the surface and
internal capacity of the cell. Two physical definitions must be kept
separate. Equation (6) may directly define a global overhead from the mean
degree. If instead every cell has local overhead $1/(k+C)$ and this quantity
is averaged, Jensen's inequality gives the exact bound

$$
\boxed{
\delta_{\rm loc}=\left\langle\frac{1}{k+C}\right\rangle
\geq
\frac{1}{\langle k\rangle+C}=\delta_{\rm mean}
}
\tag{7a}
$$

with a strict inequality for nonzero degree variance. With
$\mu=\langle k\rangle$, the formal moment expansion in its domain of
validity is

$$
\delta_{\rm loc}
=\frac{1}{\mu+C}
+\frac{\operatorname{Var}(k)}{(\mu+C)^3}
-\frac{\langle(k-\mu)^3\rangle}{(\mu+C)^4}+\cdots.
\tag{7b}
$$

For $\mu=48\pi^2/35+2$ and $C=28$,
$\delta_{\rm mean}=0.0229697827528021$, while the coefficient of the leading
absolute correction is
$1/(\mu+C)^3=1.2119108203766\times10^{-5}$ per unit
$\operatorname{Var}(k)$. A numerical $\delta_{\rm loc}$ does not follow
without the full degree distribution of the growing network or sufficiently
controlled moments. The historical $C=0$ measurement
$\langle1/k\rangle=0.0701>1/\langle k\rangle=0.0647$ only shows that the
fluctuation effect can be nonzero; it cannot be imported as the correction
for $C=28$ or for a dynamically dividing network.

**Status in v3.18:** `CONDITIONAL`. The arithmetic of equation (6) is exact
once `<k>` and `C` are accepted, but identifying it with a universal
cosmological dissipation parameter is not independently proved. If it is an
average of local overhead, `0.02297` is only the Jensen lower bound; if (6)
defines a global overhead from the mean degree, that is a different
coarse-graining hypothesis. V3.18 does not decide between them.
