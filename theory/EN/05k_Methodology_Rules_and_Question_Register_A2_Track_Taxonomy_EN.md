# REGISTER 05 — EN correction of the new A2-track taxonomy

**Date:** 2026-07-13  
**Status:** binding addendum; AR1–AR9 are unchanged

## Duplicate check

AR2 governs track birth and death but does not specify when a change of
physical mechanism requires a new peer-level number. AR10 adds precisely
this missing taxonomic condition; it does not duplicate AR2 or AR3's
non-deletion rule.

## AR10 — A change of physical mechanism creates a new peer-level track

Once a track has a concrete action or a closed equation system, a change of
action, microscopic carrier, production law, transfer type, or background
flow must receive a new A1/A2-Kn identifier. A slash subtrack is allowed only
when the mechanism, ledger, and null limits are shared. A concrete dead track
must not be used as an umbrella parent for physically different replacements.

When numbering is corrected, historical names are not deleted. They remain
explicit aliases with the reason for their restriction, preserving links,
scripts, and hashes.

## Restriction of older formulations

The labels `K5/K3a`, `K5/K4a`, `K5/K2a`, and `K5/K6` are restricted by the
later audit to historical aliases. This is not a death verdict for their
physical hypotheses. The error was the implication that they were children
of the concrete conformal K5. The new mechanisms actually arose from causes
C1–C6 in the joint autopsy of A2-K1 through A2-K5 and are peer-level tracks
A2-K6 through A2-K10.

The active next step is named **A2-K6.1**, not K3a.1.
