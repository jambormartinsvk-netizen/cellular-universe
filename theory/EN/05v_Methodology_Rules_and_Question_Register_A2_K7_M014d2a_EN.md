## Duplicate check

AR21 separates correlation time from interaction rate. It does not define
the scope of a soft-graviton no-go or require leading `hT` and curvature
operators to branch. AR22 is therefore not a duplicate.

## AR22 — A soft no-go must not exceed the audited operator class

A verdict based on a soft Ward identity must state whether it tests the
leading `h_mn T^mn` coupling or higher-derivative curvature operators.
Excluding an enhanced/non-universal leading coupling does not by itself
exclude every diffeomorphism-invariant higher-derivative term. Such terms
require a separate track, on-shell reduction, and EFT-cutoff test. An
over-broad working conclusion is retained with an erratum, not silently
overwritten.

## Q49 — Did the enhanced leading spin-2 coupling K1b1 survive?

**Status:** `NO — DEAD M-014d2a, Maximum depth 41/100.`

The rate would require `M_eff<=1.77–1.92 keV` today, a coupling roughly
`1.3e24` stronger, and universal `G_eff~1e48 G_N`. A species-dependent
enhancement of the leading massless-spin-2 coupling fails soft
universality. Curvature subtrack K1b2 remains open; K7 remains `30/100`.

### Limitation of the older formulation

“A new non-gravitational steam coupling” now branches: leading soft K1b1 is
dead; curvature K1b2 is undecided; a new non-spin-2 carrier belongs to K1c.
These options must not be merged again.
