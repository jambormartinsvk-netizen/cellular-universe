## Duplication check

AR28 requires a primordial kill test to belong to the complete regular
basis. It does not specify in which variables regularity is judged or what
happens when a species extension adds velocity-isocurvature modes with
singular Newtonian potentials. AR31 adds this missing gauge condition; it
neither replaces nor duplicates AR28.

## AR31 — Primordial regularity is judged gauge-invariantly

A track or mode must not be killed merely because a potential or velocity
variable diverges in one gauge when frame-invariant physical quantities and
a suitable regular gauge remain finite.

Before a verdict one must:

1. identify gauge-invariant curvature, entropy, and relative-velocity
   quantities;
2. construct the initial series in a gauge where the mode is regular;
3. perform an explicit gauge map only at finite time;
4. distinguish a gauge singularity from divergence of an invariant tensor or
   physical transfer;
5. re-audit the dimension of the complete basis after adding a species.

An earlier basis remains complete only for its explicitly declared species
and closure system. It does not automatically inherit the word “complete”
after one fluid is split into several kinetic species.
