## AR15 — Reconstructing a coefficient is not deriving its microphysics

When a background field is monotonic, any required time function can be
formally rewritten as a function of that field. Such a reconstructed
`Upsilon(phi)` or `Gamma(phi)` passes only algebraic existence. A
microphysical gate passes only when a concrete local action, spectral
density, or collision integral independently fixes:

- the retarded/memory kernel;
- the sign and magnitude of the local limit;
- the noise correlator or a reason for its absence;
- the bath energy, pressure, and entropy;
- the validity range of the Markovian approximation.

A coefficient obtained by inverting the desired `H(z)` must not increase a
physical track's score.
