## AR68 — A PASS requires a closed constraint passport

Every future G1–G9 gate and each A3/A4 working station must have a separate
constraint passport before calculation. The passport records physical laws,
limits, the null limit, a ban on hidden parameters, explicit PASS/STOP/REVIEW
criteria, and evidence artifacts. A partial test must not be called a full
PASS if any mandatory constraint is missing or `REVIEW_BLOCKED`.

AR68 is an organisational and epistemic rule: it adds no physical assumption
and does not reclassify older results. For earlier gates, a passport is
reconstructed before their next scoring or extension.

The canonical SK constraint register is
`Questions/00_GATE_AND_STATION_CONSTRAINT_LEDGER_SK.md`.
