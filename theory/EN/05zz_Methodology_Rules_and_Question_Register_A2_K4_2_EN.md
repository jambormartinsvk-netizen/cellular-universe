`±1/sqrt(3)`. The interaction enters only at lower order `k^0`, does not
change the characteristics, and has the correct `lambda=0` limit. The
propagating fuel and radiation blocks are diagonalizable with positive
effective kinetic and gradient signs.

The zero-speed CDM and baryon blocks are pressureless-dust Jordan blocks.
The same defect exists at `lambda=0`; it is not a new K4 instability and is
retained as a limitation of the effective-fluid approximation.

`00` residual was `4.41484e-8`, and all three q=300 convergence gates passed.
At every q, K4 had a smaller `T_max` than its null limit, so the large
subhorizon transfer is not an interaction-driven high-k explosion.

## What Q56 does not prove

Q56 does not establish a microscopic action, a fundamental UV no-ghost
theorem, a full photon/neutrino Boltzmann hierarchy, CMB agreement, or the
correct `S8`. Historical M-011 is retained, but K4.1 and K4.2 restrict its
general scope to the old non-regular velocity seed and an invalid
interpretation of reference gain as absolute divergence.
