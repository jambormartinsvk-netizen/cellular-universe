## AR38 — An incomplete source vector is not a physical verdict

If the left-null/Bianchi identities fail after inserting only one stress-energy component, the track must not be declared dead until all compulsory terms generated by the same background expansion have been included: changes in `Omega_A`, `Hconf_x/Hconf`, gradient factors, Euler equations, metric cross terms, and the required hierarchy. An algebraic completion may be used only as a diagnostic witness; a physical PASS requires derivation from the model without a new fitted parameter.

When several Puiseux powers occur, the system must be solved in ascending order. An earlier compensated relative mode must not be skipped merely because its total density or momentum vanishes at the first moment.

## Q65 — Is K4 physically compatible after BR3B?

**Status: PARTLY ANSWERED, OPEN FOR G7.**

The common `h_x` sector is physically compatible: the compulsory background/Euler completion gives two exact Bianchi zeros in all AD/CDI/BI/NID/NIV modes and adds no fitted parameter. NID/NIV contain earlier compensated sectors whose neutrino shear and `l>=3` hierarchy have not yet been solved. K4 remains alive at 60/100; G7 is not closed.

The older statement from script 97 that the isolated fuel source is rank-incompatible remains valid only for that deliberately incomplete ansatz. Audits 98–100 restricted its interpretation: it is not evidence that the complete K4 track is dead.
