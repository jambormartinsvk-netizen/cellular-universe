## AR41 — A legacy oracle must pass a species-local audit

When a full-rank system with small residuals disagrees with an older
comparison result, the track must not be killed from that disagreement alone.
The difference must first be localized by equation row, and the comparison
oracle must be checked for the correct variable, sign, gauge, and species.

In a Boltzmann hierarchy, a species-local equation must use the perturbation
of the same component unless an explicit interaction term proves otherwise.
In particular, the shear of a free-streaming component uses that component's
own velocity, not the photon velocity. A small matrix residual with an
incorrect source proves only that the supplied matrix was solved correctly;
it does not validate the physical source mapping.

The older artifact is retained. Its surviving scope, the reason for its
restriction, and the new audited artifact superseding the faulty oracle must
all be recorded.
