## AR40 — A Puiseux sector list must be closed under background powers

Before an ascending Puiseux chain is declared complete, its power list must
be closed under sums with every non-zero background order that can enter the
equations up to the target depth. In particular, a non-zero matter correction
`epsilon_m ~ a` generates a candidate `p+j+1` from every `p+j` sector, while
the gradient `k^2/Hconf^2 ~ a^2` generates `p+j+2`.

A pure-radiation PASS must not be inherited at `epsilon_m != 0` until the
mixed matter/fuel sectors, carried baryon/CDM variables, and the complete
species/Einstein source vector have been checked. The older PASS is not
deleted; its scope is explicitly restricted.
