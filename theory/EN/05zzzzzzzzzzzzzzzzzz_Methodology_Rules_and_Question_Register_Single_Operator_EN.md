# Addendum to 05 — single-operator requirement (EN)

## AR47 — naming a “single operator” does not specify an operator

A common origin of production and dissipation is established only by a concrete action, matrix element, or complete collision kernel from which the relevant moments and backreaction are derived. The sum of two independent phenomenological terms does not become one operator by receiving one name.

The same background and the same zeroth moment can have different linear momentum transfer. Therefore a relation between production and relaxation rates must not be inserted or fitted without a microphysical derivation.

## Q73 — is there a concrete K9 kernel linking production, scattering, and noise?

**Status:** OPEN.

It must reproduce the A1 transfer, keep constant ash mass, and derive a nonzero elastic first moment plus its noise/memory without an independent parameter added to lower `S8`.

## Limitation of the older wording

The older statement that “one process determines both production and braking” is a target hypothesis, not a result. K9 currently registers a G1 design class; it has not passed G2.
