## Duplicate check

AR17 requires positive noise and AR20 separates bath types. Neither rule
explicitly distinguishes a short correlation/oscillation scale from the
size of the interaction rate and transport coefficient. AR21 is therefore
not a duplicate.

## AR21 — `T/H >> 1` does not prove sufficient dissipation

Separation of a microscopic frequency or correlation time from the Hubble
scale is only a condition for a local/Markov approximation. The interaction
rate and the magnitude of the derived transport coefficient must be tested
separately. A thermal-shaped distribution retained after decoupling must
not be confused with active thermalisation of the system under study.

## Q48 — Can the registered graviton steam with gravity-only coupling be the K7 bath?

**Status:** `NO — DEAD M-014d1, Maximum depth 40/100.`

At `T_0=0.905 K`, `T/H` is enormous, but the optimistic estimate
`Gamma_grav~T^5/Mbar_Pl^4` gives `Gamma/H=3.80e-87` at recombination and
`5.80e-98` today. It misses the exact K7 source by at least 83–85 orders of
magnitude even at the best historical point. K1a is dead; script 60 and its
output are retained.

The older label “thermal graviton relic” remains only a statement about
origin/distribution shape and is itself conditional on Q18/Q23. The later
audit excludes silently extending it to “a local active K7 bath” with only
gravitational coupling. A new non-gravitational coupling is the separate
K1b subtrack.
