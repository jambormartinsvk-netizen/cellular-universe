## 4. Background correction: `K_MPC=0.05` is not a cosmological background scale

### v3.17 / early runner form

A fixed `K_MPC=0.05` and an implicit `Phi=1` entered expressions containing
the perturbative variable

```text
z = k a / [H0 sqrt(Omega_r0)].
```

Used directly in a homogeneous density term, this allowed the reconstructed
FLRW background to depend on the Fourier mode selected by the runner.

### v3.18 correction

The accepted provenance identifies `k` as a perturbative Fourier mode or
pivot normalization, not a derived cell-network scale, ash velocity, or
constant of nature. A homogeneous FLRW background must satisfy

```text
partial H_FLRW(a,k) / partial k = 0.
```

The early integration amplitude therefore transforms as

```text
Phi(k) = A_f [H0 sqrt(Omega_r0) / k]^p,
p = 4 - 3 delta,
```

which gives the exact cancellation

```text
Phi(k) z^p = A_f a^p.
```

For the frozen A1 input point

```text

h=0.6637, flat closure, x_reference=-18,
```

the conditional bookkeeping normalization is

```text
A_f = 7809.270101963506.
```

This is a reproduced normalization of that frozen closure. It is not a new
fit, microphysical derivation, constant of nature, completed K4 background,
or CLASS/CAMB result.

## 6. Division overhead and cell capacity are more carefully scoped

The mean-field benchmark remains

```text
delta_mean = 1 / (<k> + C) = 0.0229697827528021.
```

V3.18 now distinguishes this from a possible local-average definition

```text
delta_loc = <1/(k+C)>.
```

For nonzero degree variance and `k+C>0`, Jensen's inequality requires

```text
delta_loc > delta_mean.
```

A numerical `delta_loc` therefore needs the degree distribution `P(k)` or
controlled moments; it cannot be inferred from the mean degree alone.

The arithmetic `C=16_gluon+8_EW+4_Higgs=28` is internally consistent in the
restored electroweak counting. The four real Higgs directions already
include the three would-be Goldstone directions and must not be added a
second time. However, identifying this bosonic count with cell capacity,
excluding fermions, and avoiding a substrate/emergence circularity remain
underived. `C=28` and `m=1/2` are mechanism readings, not theorems.

## 11. Methodology and falsifiability changes

V3.18 adds an explicit audit and falsification discipline:

- exact laws and conservation identities have exact tolerance within their
  declared mathematical scope;
- observational exclusions require a model-to-observable map, uncertainties,
  covariance, systematics, and the declared likelihood;
- unknown functions are represented by admissible sets before a particular
  ansatz is chosen;
- alternative tracks form a finite version-frozen union, not an
  intersection;
- a waiting backup is not evidence of survival, but it prevents premature
  parent-theory death until the alternatives are proved exhaustive;
- technical errors, timeouts, solver tolerances, script counts, and depth
  scores have no physical death reach;
- dead tracks, scripts, raw outputs, and exact reasons are retained so a
  failed formulation is not silently retried;
- calculation lineage must be audited before a downstream result can inherit
  an upstream equation or normalization;
- every externally reusable milestone can be bound to exact hashes and
  submitted independently to multiple auditors.

The new EC01–EC43 register separates:

- exact physical laws and guards;
- observational survival targets;
- conditional model outputs;
- mechanism readings;
- calibration benchmarks;
- open conditions with no current kill window.

It also records what each failure can kill and the mandatory claim that must
not be inferred from the row.

## 13. What remains unchanged

are investigated as emergent descriptions of a discrete, nonequilibrium
causal cellular network. The programme still seeks one physical account of
accelerated expansion, matter/ash/steam production, propagation, scars,

does not declare QCTS experimentally confirmed and does not replace general
relativity, quantum field theory, the Standard Model, or ΛCDM in their
validated domains.
